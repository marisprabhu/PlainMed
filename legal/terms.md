## 1. What PlainMed is

PlainMed reads a laboratory report you provide and explains, in plain
language, what the report says. Every explanation is linked to the line of
your report it came from, so you can check it.

## 2. What PlainMed is not

**PlainMed does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment
recommendations.** It describes what your report contains. It does not tell
you what is wrong with you, what to do about it, or whether a result is good
or bad for you specifically.

Reference ranges differ between laboratories and between people. A result
inside a range is not a statement that you are healthy, and a result outside
one is not a statement that you are unwell. Only a qualified clinician who
knows your history can interpret your results.

**If you believe you have a medical emergency, contact emergency services
immediately. Do not use PlainMed.**

## 3. It can be wrong

PlainMed uses automated text recognition and an AI model. Both make
mistakes. It may misread a number, miss a result entirely, or fail to
recognise part of your report.

This is why PlainMed shows you what it read and asks you to confirm it
before explaining anything, and why it highlights values it was unsure
about. **You are responsible for checking that what PlainMed read matches
your report.** Do not rely on PlainMed's reading without comparing it to the
original document.

## 4. Who may use it

You must be 18 or older. You may use PlainMed only for reports that are
yours, or for someone you are legally authorised to act for.
